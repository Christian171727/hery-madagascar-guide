Your Local Tour Guide in Madagascar

A local guide does more than show you a destination. A good guided experience helps you understand the landscapes, wildlife, traditions and everyday life that make a place special.

HERY Madagascar Local Guide is based in Morondava and focuses on nature, wildlife observation, photography, astrophotography, cultural discovery and conservation-minded travel. The approach is personal: start with your interests, then discuss how to experience the places you wish to visit.

Why travel with a local guide?

Local insight can make planning easier and help visitors make informed choices about destinations and activities. It can also encourage respectful visits to natural areas and interactions with local communities.

Based in Morondava and the Menabe region

Morondava opens the door to famous western Madagascar landscapes. The Avenue of the Baobabs, Kirindy Forest and routes toward Tsingy de Bemaraha are among the experiences travelers often ask about.

Photography, wildlife and cultural discovery

HERY's guiding interests bring together outdoor experiences and observation of Madagascar's remarkable biodiversity. If you are a photographer, nature enthusiast or independent traveler, your plan can focus on the places and subjects that matter most to you.

What can your experience include?

  • Personalized local guiding
  • Private nature and cultural excursions
  • Wildlife and biodiversity discovery
  • Photography-oriented outings
  • Morondava and Menabe local experiences
  • Help discussing a custom itinerary

Good to know: HERY's services are tailored to individual requests. Ask directly about locations, dates, guiding scope and travel arrangements before confirming.
