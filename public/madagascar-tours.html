DISCOVER WITH HERY

Madagascar Tours, Guided by Local Knowledge

Madagascar is a place to explore slowly. Different regions reveal striking landscapes, endemic wildlife and communities with their own traditions. HERY is based in Morondava, in the Menabe region, and helps travelers explore Madagascar through local knowledge and personalized guiding.

Whether your interests are wildlife, nature photography, local life or extraordinary landscapes, your tour can be planned around your priorities, available time and the conditions of the places you want to visit.

Private tours designed around your interests

Choose the kind of experience you want rather than following a fixed itinerary. Your travel plan can focus on nature, photography, cultural encounters or a combination of different experiences. HERY can discuss destinations, practical arrangements and the approach that best suits your group.

Discover western Madagascar

Morondava and Menabe are a rewarding starting point for discovering the Avenue of the Baobabs, the wildlife-rich Kirindy Forest and journeys toward Tsingy de Bemaraha. Each place offers a different perspective on the environment and landscapes of the west.

Nature, wildlife and photography

Wildlife sightings always depend on nature, season and conditions. Guided observation emphasizes patience, appropriate distances and respect for habitats. Travelers interested in photography and astrophotography can discuss light, timing and location before planning a visit.

What can your experience include?

  • Private guiding and itinerary discussion
  • Morondava and Menabe excursions
  • Avenue of the Baobabs landscapes
  • Kirindy Forest wildlife observation
  • Tsingy de Bemaraha travel planning
  • Nature photography and astrophotography experiences

Good to know: Tour routes, park access, transfers, prices and availability should always be confirmed with HERY before your trip.

BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

Frequently asked questions

Can I arrange a private Madagascar tour?

Yes. Contact HERY with your preferred destinations, travel dates and interests to discuss a personalized guided experience.

Which areas should I consider in western Madagascar?

Many visitors explore Morondava, the Avenue of the Baobabs, Kirindy Forest and, when conditions permit, Tsingy de Bemaraha.

Are transport and park fees included?

Inclusions depend on the particular arrangement. Confirm transport, accommodation, park fees and other costs directly with HERY before booking.
